EMMYCRYPT Research #005

FOLLOW THE CAPITAL

Bitcoin Market Structure & Capital-Flow Investigation

Research snapshot: August 2026

Research question

Does the available evidence support a durable Bitcoin recovery, and what do capital flows, holder supply, derivatives positioning, liquidity, macro conditions and corporate treasury behaviour actually establish?

Executive finding

Bitcoin's August recovery is supported by several independent evidence streams, but the evidence does not yet establish a fully confirmed expansion regime.

The strongest evidence is improved U.S. spot Bitcoin ETF flows and a rebound accompanied by substantial deleveraging rather than a simple build-up of new long leverage.

Key market test

The central market-structure issue is the $83K–$86K supply band, where approximately 1.05M BTC is concentrated around a long-term-holder cost-basis zone. The key test is whether fresh demand can absorb breakeven and profit-taking supply.

Capital flows and leverage

The research records a strong period of positive ETF net creation followed by a negative session, while cited derivatives analysis reports that 85% of measured liquidations across the squeeze window were short-side and futures open interest fell 11% in coin terms.

Research standard

Exchange transfers are not automatically treated as sales, liquidations are not equivalent to voluntary buying, and macro narratives are not treated as causal proof without corroboration.

Conclusion

Bitcoin has entered a materially stronger recovery regime, and the next decisive evidence will be whether demand can absorb the first major long-term-holder supply wall without a deterioration in capital flows or leverage quality.
