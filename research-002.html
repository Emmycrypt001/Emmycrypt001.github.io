EMMYCRYPT Research #002

AAVE: What Actually Drives the Protocol?

Focus: Protocol activity, economics, tokenomics, competition, value capture and risk.

Research question

How strong is Aave's underlying protocol activity, and how directly is that activity connected to AAVE value capture?

Key finding

Aave demonstrates strong underlying protocol activity within the research comparison set, but protocol growth does not automatically create proportional AAVE demand. The connection between protocol activity and token value capture is indirect and governance-dependent.

What the evidence supports

The research examines TVL, borrowing, utilization, gross fees, protocol revenue, treasury activity, emissions, governance, GHO, competition and risk. It deliberately separates protocol performance from token economics.

Conclusion

Protocol success should not automatically be confused with token value capture. The research identifies the variables that could change the thesis and the indicators EMMYCRYPT should monitor.
