How I Work

I start with a question, collect relevant evidence, analyze the activity, verify important findings and document what the evidence actually supports.

  1. Identify the research or data question.
  2. Collect and structure relevant information.
  3. Analyze activity, data and underlying mechanics.
  4. Verify important findings against reliable sources.
  5. Document evidence, interpretation and limitations.
