What I Research

My work focuses on questions that require looking beyond headlines and simple market narratives.

  • DeFi protocols and ecosystems — how protocols generate activity, attract liquidity and capture value.
  • On-chain activity and wallet behavior — what transactions, wallet movements and flows can actually establish.
  • Tokenomics and token distribution — how supply, incentives, allocation and emissions can affect a protocol.
  • Protocol fundamentals — how products work, where activity comes from and what the underlying data shows.
  • Capital flows and market intelligence — where capital is moving and what those movements may reveal.
  • Ethereum and Layer-2 ecosystems — activity, liquidity, adoption and the relationship between Ethereum and lower-cost execution networks.
  • Solana ecosystem activity — token movements, wallet activity and on-chain market behavior.
  • Risk and opportunity analysis — separating what the evidence supports from what remains uncertain.

How I Work

I start with a question, collect the relevant evidence, analyze the activity, verify important findings and document what the evidence actually supports.

  1. Identify the research question.
  2. Collect relevant on-chain and market data.
  3. Analyze activity, protocol mechanics and market behavior.
  4. Verify important findings against reliable sources.
  5. Document the evidence, interpretation and limitations.

Research Standard

If the evidence does not prove a claim, I don't present the claim as fact.

Where possible, I distinguish between directly verifiable blockchain activity, source-reported information, calculated figures, analytical interpretation, and uncertainty.

A transaction can show that assets moved. It does not automatically explain why they moved.
