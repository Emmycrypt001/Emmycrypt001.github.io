Decision Framework

  1. Research before allocation — establish what the evidence actually supports.
  2. Conviction determines position size — stronger independent evidence can justify greater exposure.
  3. Preserve optionality — reserves remain available when evidence changes.
  4. Risk before return — invalidation and downside are considered before upside.
  5. Evidence can change the portfolio — allocation is not fixed when the underlying thesis changes.
