EMMYCRYPT Research #004

Uniswap v4: Meaningful Adoption, or Just More Liquidity on a New Architecture?

Category: Protocol / Market Investigation

The question

Is Uniswap v4's new architecture translating into meaningful economic activity, or are we mainly seeing existing Uniswap trading move onto newer infrastructure?

What the data shows

The research reports approximately $961.7M in v4 TVL and approximately $22.18B in 30-day trading volume for the measured period, demonstrating meaningful economic activity.

Key finding

The evidence supports adoption, but it does not establish that v4's distinctive architecture caused the reported activity. Ethereum accounts for approximately 74.2% of reported v4 TVL in the research snapshot.

Hooks

Hooks are a major architectural capability, but the verified evidence reviewed does not reliably quantify hook adoption, hook-related volume or their incremental economic impact.

Conclusion

Uniswap v4 has meaningful usage, but the available evidence is not yet sufficient to attribute that usage directly to its distinctive architectural features.
