EMMYCRYPT Research #003

FOLLOW THE MONEY

An On-Chain Wallet Investigation — Solana Ecosystem

Research question: Are significant Solana token flows showing accumulation, distribution, or a mixed pattern — and what can the available on-chain evidence actually establish?

Key finding

The evidence supports a mixed flow pattern. Some reported activity points toward increased exchange-side liquidity and possible distribution, while very large amounts of SOL also moved away from Binance to unidentified public addresses.

Evidence standard

EMMYCRYPT separates directly verifiable blockchain transactions, source-reported evidence and analytical interpretation. A wallet receiving SOL does not prove a purchase, and an exchange deposit does not prove a sale.

Conclusion

The strongest evidence establishes significant capital movement, not the intentions of unidentified wallet owners. Transfer is not automatically trade.
