EMMYCRYPT Research #005

FOLLOW THE CAPITAL

Bitcoin Market Structure & Capital-Flow Investigation

Research snapshot: August 2026

Research Question

Does the available evidence support a durable Bitcoin recovery, and what do capital flows, holder supply, derivatives positioning, liquidity, macro conditions and corporate treasury behaviour actually establish?

Executive Summary

The research finds a materially stronger Bitcoin recovery regime, but not yet a fully confirmed expansion regime.

Key Evidence

  • U.S. spot Bitcoin ETF flows strengthened during the recovery, including nine consecutive positive sessions in the cited series before an August 28 outflow.
  • A cited derivatives analysis reported that 85% of measured liquidations across the squeeze window were short-side.
  • Futures open interest fell 11% in coin terms during the squeeze window.
  • Approximately 1.05M BTC is concentrated around the $83K–$86K long-term-holder cost-basis zone.
  • Approximately 1.44M BTC is identified around the $62K–$65K lower cost-basis cluster.

Market Structure Test

The central question is whether fresh demand can absorb breakeven and profit-taking supply around $83K–$86K after the initial short-squeeze fuel has faded.

Capital Flows & Liquidity

The research treats ETF creations, exchange supply, realized capitalization and stablecoin capitalization as separate evidence streams. Stablecoin growth represents potential purchasing power, not proof of BTC demand. Exchange transfers are treated as supply context, not proof of selling intent.

Corporate Treasury Behaviour

The investigation examines documented corporate BTC sales and financing activity and concludes that treasury demand is not necessarily a permanent one-way source of buying; financing conditions can create both demand and potential supply.

Monitoring Framework

Future updates should monitor ETF flows, the $83K–$86K supply band, spot versus derivatives leadership, funding and open interest, long-term-holder distribution, macro conditions and corporate treasury activity. Convergence across independent categories matters more than any single indicator.

Core Finding

Bitcoin has entered a materially stronger recovery regime, and the next decisive evidence will be whether demand can absorb the first major long-term-holder supply wall without a deterioration in capital flows or leverage quality.
