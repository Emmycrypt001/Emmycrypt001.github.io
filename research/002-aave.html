EMMYCRYPT Research #002

AAVE: What Actually Drives the Protocol?

Focus: Protocol activity, economics, tokenomics, competition, value capture and risk.

Research Question

How strong is Aave's underlying protocol activity, and how directly is that activity connected to AAVE value capture?

Research Snapshot

  • TVL: approximately $14.815B
  • Active loans: approximately $11.419B
  • Calculated utilization: approximately 77.0%
  • 30-day gross fees: approximately $29.32M
  • 30-day protocol revenue: approximately $3.95M

Key Finding

Aave demonstrates substantial protocol activity, but protocol growth does not automatically translate into proportional AAVE value capture. The connection is indirect and governance-dependent.

Competitive Analysis

The investigation compares Aave with Morpho Blue and Compound V3, examining TVL, borrowing, chains, fees and protocol revenue. The comparison is directional rather than perfectly like-for-like because the architectures differ.

Risk Areas

The research examines smart contracts, oracles, collateral, liquidity, bridges and external dependencies, governance, competition and regulation. It also considers bad-debt risk and collateral incidents.

Research Standard

If the evidence does not prove a claim, I don't present the claim as fact.

The investigation distinguishes observable protocol activity, source-reported information, calculated figures, analytical interpretation, uncertainty and limitations.
