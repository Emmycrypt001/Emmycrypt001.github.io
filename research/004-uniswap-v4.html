EMMYCRYPT Research #004

Uniswap v4: Meaningful Adoption, or Just More Liquidity on a New Architecture?

Research period: January 1, 2026 – August 22, 2026
Category: Protocol / Market Investigation

The Question

Is Uniswap v4's new architecture translating into meaningful economic activity, or are we mainly seeing existing Uniswap trading move onto newer infrastructure?

What the Data Shows

  • Approximately $961.7M in reported v4 TVL as of August 22, 2026.
  • Approximately $22.18B in 30-day trading volume for July 23–August 22, 2026.
  • Approximately $37.85M in 30-day fees for the same period.
  • Ethereum represented approximately 74.2% of reported v4 TVL.

Hooks & Architecture

The research examines singleton architecture, flash accounting and hooks. Hooks enable custom logic around pool operations, but the verified evidence reviewed did not establish their percentage of pools, trading volume or fees.

Key Finding

The data clearly supports meaningful adoption. It does not, by itself, establish that v4's distinctive architecture caused the reported economic activity.

What the Evidence Does Not Prove

  • That v4 architecture caused the reported trading volume
  • That hooks drive a significant share of v4 activity
  • That v4 has already displaced previous Uniswap versions
  • That v4 activity necessarily translates into UNI token performance

Conclusion

Uniswap v4 has meaningful usage, but the available evidence is not yet sufficient to attribute that usage directly to its distinctive architectural features.
