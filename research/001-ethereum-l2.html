EMMYCRYPT Research #001

Ethereum DeFi: Is Activity Moving to L2s While Liquidity Stays on Mainnet?

Research focus: DeFi activity, Ethereum liquidity, Layer-2 activity, stablecoin supply, transaction execution and capital concentration.

Research Question

Is DeFi activity moving toward lower-cost execution networks while capital remains concentrated on Ethereum mainnet?

Key Finding

The investigation identifies an important difference between where blockchain execution occurs and where capital is concentrated. Ethereum can retain substantial DeFi liquidity and stablecoin supply while lower-cost networks record significant transaction activity.

Execution location and capital concentration should not be treated as the same measurement.

What the Evidence Does Not Prove

  • Permanent migration of users away from Ethereum
  • Permanent migration of economic value away from Ethereum
  • That higher transaction activity automatically represents greater economic value
  • The future direction of Ethereum or its L2 ecosystem

Research Approach

The investigation compares network activity with liquidity and capital indicators, separates observable evidence from interpretation, and documents what the evidence supports and what remains uncertain.

Conclusion

The evidence suggests that execution can increasingly occur on lower-cost networks while Ethereum remains an important location for capital and liquidity. This makes the distinction between activity, liquidity and capital concentration important when evaluating Ethereum's evolving DeFi ecosystem.
