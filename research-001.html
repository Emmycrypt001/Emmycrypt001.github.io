EMMYCRYPT Research #001

Ethereum DeFi: Is Activity Moving to L2s While Liquidity Stays on Mainnet?

Research question: Is DeFi activity moving toward lower-cost execution networks while capital remains concentrated on Ethereum mainnet?

Research focus

This investigation examines DeFi activity, Ethereum mainnet liquidity, Layer-2 activity, stablecoin supply, transaction execution and capital concentration.

Key finding

The evidence indicates a difference between where capital is concentrated and where blockchain execution occurs. Ethereum can retain substantial DeFi liquidity and stablecoin supply while lower-cost networks record significant transaction activity.

Conclusion

Execution location and capital concentration should not be treated as the same measurement. Activity can increasingly occur on lower-cost execution networks while Ethereum remains an important location for capital and liquidity.
